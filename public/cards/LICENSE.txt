Four-index SVG playing-card faces generated from Adrian Kennard's
public-domain SVG playing-card project:
https://www.me.uk/cards/
https://codeberg.org/RevK/SVG-playing-cards

The author releases the designs under the CC0 Public Domain licence.
The selected deck uses standard two-colour suits and integrated indices
in all four corners.

The files are bundled locally so the game does not contact third-party servers.
